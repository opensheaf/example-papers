# Papers of the journal Quantum (volume 7, 2023), under CC BY 4.0

Three papers published in [Quantum](https://quantum-journal.org), an
open-access journal that publishes under CC BY 4.0 and whose papers are on
arXiv. Every arXiv version of each was checked to be under CC BY 4.0 (in
`version-licences.json`, read from each version's arXiv page); the versions of
record are CC BY 4.0 as stated by the journal and in Crossref
(`crossref.json`). They are redistributed here unchanged, with attribution:

- Christoph Hirche, *Quantum Network Discrimination*, Quantum 7, 1064 (2023),
  doi:10.22331/q-2023-07-25-1064, arXiv:2103.02404 (v1, v2).
- Tanmay Singal, Che Chiang, Eugene Hsu, Eunsang Kim, Hsi-Sheng Goan,
  Min-Hsiu Hsieh, *Counting stabiliser codes for arbitrary dimension*,
  Quantum 7, 1048 (2023), doi:10.22331/q-2023-07-06-1048, arXiv:2209.01449
  (v1, v2).
- Evan Peters, Maria Schuld, *Generalization despite overfitting in quantum
  machine learning models*, Quantum 7, 1210 (2023),
  doi:10.22331/q-2023-12-20-1210, arXiv:2209.05523 (v1, v2).

Folders: `papers/` (arXiv records, every version's PDF and source, as written
by `osheaf arxiv fetch`), `identifiers.json` (the object identifiers used for
the import), `version-of-record/` (the journal's PDFs, downloaded from
quantum-journal.org on the date in `downloaded-on.txt`, with `SHA256SUMS`).
The deposits of the versions of record are in `../deposits/quantum-vor.yaml`.

They serve an unofficial demonstration (`examples/journals.sh` of the
[collection template](https://github.com/opensheaf/collection-template)):
nothing here is made by or for Quantum.
